# Campaign research: Prophecies, pre-Searing through The Frost Gate

Research notes for the first campaign (2026-09-26, from wiki.guildwars.com). For each
mission: what happens in the MMO, then how it might become an IA mission. After that,
the enemy roster, with suggested IA translations.

**Everything under "IA ideas" is a suggestion for the designer, not a decision.**
Setting background is in `docs/setting.md`.

## Campaign shape

The MMO's story order is: **Ascalon Academy** (pre-Searing) → *the Searing* → **The Great
Northern Wall** → **Fort Ranik** → **Ruins of Surmia** → **Nolani Academy** → *Rurik
banished; refugees head for the mountains* → **Borlis Pass** → **The Frost Gate**.

That's an intro, five story missions and a finale: about the size of an IA
mini-campaign or a short full campaign. It breaks naturally into two acts:

| Act | Missions | Adversary | Enemy level (MMO) | Suggested threat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | Ascalon Academy | Grawl, one Charr | 1–6 | lowest |
| I: Ascalon | Great Northern Wall, Fort Ranik, Ruins of Surmia, Nolani Academy (act finale vs Bonfaaz) | Charr, devourers, gargoyles | 5–10 | rising |
| II: Shiverpeaks | Borlis Pass, The Frost Gate (finale) | Stone Summit, ice creatures, centaurs | 9–14 | high |

- **Rurik** is a recurring ally in almost every mission. He's a natural IA ally card,
  and often the protect or escort target.
- **The finale is a tragedy:** Rurik dies at the Frost Gate as the refugees escape. One
  IA option is a finale where the heroes win by getting the refugees through, and
  Rurik's survival changes the ending text.
- **Side-mission sources:**
  - Pre-Searing quests (*A Mesmer's Burden*, the start of Old Mac's story).
  - *Defend the Wall*.
  - *The Way is Blocked* (clear a Stone Summit blockade led by the mesmer boss Inar
    Frostbite).
  - Each mission's bonus objective (listed below).
  - Hero-personal hooks (see `docs/setting.md`).

## Missions

### Intro: Ascalon Academy (pre-Searing)

- **MMO:** Rurik inducts the heroes into the Ascalon Vanguard. They go through the
  Catacombs to a bunker behind the Citadel to kill the Charr infiltrator **Vatlaaw
  Doomtooth** (a Level 6 ranger), fighting Grawl warriors, rangers, longspears and
  shamans. Rurik could beat everyone on his own. Finishing the mission triggers the
  Searing.
- **IA ideas:**
  - A gentle intro mission, like IA's *Aftermath*: weak Grawl groups, with Vatlaaw as a
    boss figure.
  - Rurik as a strong free ally, so the heroes learn the rules with a safety net.
  - End on the Searing as story text. It could set the campaign's "post-Searing" state:
    new agenda sets, harsher terrain.

### 1. The Great Northern Wall

- **MMO:**
  - **Primary:** scout north of the Wall to find Bonfaaz's army, report to Captain
    Calhaan, then retreat. The Wall falls back to Fort Ranik.
  - **Bonus:** recover 4 pieces of the ghost Kilnn Testibrie's armour and return them to
    his tomb.
  - **Map:** two lever-operated gates (one controlled by each side), and purple tar
    pools that apply *Tar*.
- **Enemies:** Charr of every profession; carrion, whiptail and plague devourers;
  shatter and flash gargoyles; Grawl.
- **IA ideas:**
  - **Scout-and-escape:** reveal points of interest (interact with or reach tokens),
    then exit before a round limit while Charr reinforcements pour in.
  - **Lever gates** become IA doors opened from terminals. The Charr-controlled gate
    could be a mission rule that lets the Adversary close it.
  - **Tar pools** become difficult terrain, perhaps also applying Weaken or Bleed.
  - **The armour pieces** become four retrievable tokens (crate-like); returning them
    all is the bonus.

### 2. Fort Ranik

- **MMO:**
  - **Primary:** push back the Charr; find three trebuchet parts (restraining bolt,
    arming crank, release lever) for Siegemaster Lormar; repair and fire the trebuchets.
  - **Bonus:** rescue captured Trooper Deeter Saberlin.
  - **Map:** canyon walls that block line of sight. The wiki's tactics note says Charr
    Stalkers are best countered by breaking line of sight behind walls.
  - Ends with Rurik vowing to march north and free the prisoners.
- **Enemies:** Charr warriors, stalkers (with Ignite Arrows), Fire Callers (with Meteor
  Shower), monks (Martyr and Overseer, with Shield of Judgment), Mind Sparks, Ash
  Walkers, Ashen Claws, and carrion and plague devourers. Six Charr bosses.
- **IA ideas:**
  - Carry the three parts back (retrieve tokens), then fire the trebuchet (interact,
    perhaps with a Strength test).
  - The trebuchet then works as a mission-controlled Blast weapon, bombarding a
    deployment point each round.
  - The rescue works as an IA-style "free the prisoner" side objective, and the rescued
    trooper becomes a temporary ally.

### 3. Ruins of Surmia

- **MMO:**
  - **Primary:** keep Rurik alive, free all the captives (including three Ascalon
    mages), reach the obelisks, cross a ravine and lower the drawbridge.
  - **Bonus:** follow the **Ember Bearers** to a Flame Temple and put out the Sacred
    Flame by killing four **Flame Keepers** at the Cauldron of Cataclysm.
  - **Hidden devourer ambushes** throughout.
  - Ends at Nolani Academy with the mouthpiece of the Stormcaller horn.
- **IA ideas:**
  - **Protect Rurik:** the mission is lost if the ally is defeated.
  - Freed captives become weak allies, like IA's mission-specific allies. Erol, an
    elementalist, joins the heroes after he's freed.
  - **Devourer ambushes suit IA's reserved groups:** face-down burrow tokens that
    deploy devourers when a hero comes near.
  - The Ember Bearers are figures carrying a flame token to a temple objective. It's
    also **Cynn's hometown**, so this could double as her personal story beat.

### 4. Nolani Academy (act I finale)

- **MMO:**
  - Sneak out of the besieged academy and ambush the Charr.
  - Escort Rurik to **Horn Hill** and sound **Stormcaller**.
  - Kill **Bonfaaz Burntfur** at Rin.
  - **Bonus:** return the Tome of the Fallen to a graveyard pedestal, which is guarded
    by hostile Spirits of the Fallen.
  - Afterwards, King Adelbern banishes Rurik.
- **Enemies:** every Charr profession, including Chaots and Flame Wielders; devourers;
  **Boulder Elementals**; gargoyles; ghosts. Six Charr bosses, the devourer boss Stank
  Reekfoul, two elemental bosses, and Bonfaaz.
- **IA ideas:**
  - A three-phase finale: breakout, then escort, then boss.
  - Stormcaller works as a mission weapon: an interact on Horn Hill that strips
    Bonfaaz's protection or damages every Charr in the area.
  - Bonfaaz as a villain card: a fire Elementalist chieftain with Lava Font or Inferno
    and a healing aura (*Aura of Restoration*).

### 5. Borlis Pass

- **MMO:**
  - Light **seven storm beacons** through a blizzard.
  - Learn the outcome of Rurik's talks with King Jalis.
  - Blow open **Maladar's Fort** with powder kegs.
  - Break the siege of **Krok's Hollow** by destroying its ballistae and killing the
    Stone Summit.
  - Light magical signal beacons with an Enchanted Torch.
  - **Bonus:** free Rornak Stonesledge, blast open an ice cave with powder kegs, and kill
    the **Ice Drake** (with Frostfire Dryders).
- **Enemies:**
  - Shiverpeak centaurs: warrior, longbow and protector, plus six bosses.
  - Stone Summit: Axe Wielder, Crusher, Beastmaster, Scout, **Dolyak Rider**, Howler,
    Sage and **Engineer**, plus five bosses.
  - **Ice Golems**, Frostfire Dryders and the Ice Drake.
- **IA ideas:**
  - **Blizzard:** a mission rule limiting line of sight to 4–5 spaces. Beacons are
    interact objectives that restore sight nearby.
  - **Powder kegs:** retrievable tokens. A hero carrying one can interact to destroy a
    door or barrier, and it explodes (Blast) if the carrier is defeated.
  - **Ballistae:** objects with Health that the heroes attack. Each ballista **fires
    only while an Engineer is adjacent**, which matches the MMO: Engineers only crew
    siege weapons.

### 6. The Frost Gate (finale)

- **MMO:**
  - Destroy four ballistae.
  - Take the Gear Lever from a dwarven fort.
  - Work **four lever mechanisms** before the Stone Summit overwhelm Rurik, while the
    refugees pass along a lower path.
  - **Bonus:** free Rornak and use a ballista to blast open doors to secret siege plans.
  - Dagnar Stonepate then kills Rurik (cinematic).
- **Enemies:** Stone Summit (Engineers, Crushers, Axe Wielders, Scouts, Dolyak Riders,
  Howlers, Sages; five bosses), Frostfire Dryders, Ice Golems, **Snow Ettins** and
  **Minotaurs**. **Dagnar** himself appears in the cinematic only (a Level 28
  elementalist riding the Ice Drake).
- **IA ideas:**
  - A finale **race**: the levers are interact objectives, with a refugee track that
    advances each round.
  - Rurik holds a chokepoint as an ally who takes damage from mission rules.
  - Dagnar on the Ice Drake as an unkillable finale threat, or a very tough villain. He
    arrives in the final rounds.
  - Heroes win when the refugees escape, and their performance decides whether Rurik
    survives.

## Enemy roster and IA translations

Already in `data/enemies/`: Carrion, Plague and Whiptail Devourers; Charr Ash Walker,
Axe Warrior/Fiend, Blade Storm, Flamecaller, Mind Spark and Stalker.

The existing designs are faithful to the MMO:
- **Stalker:** Ignite Arrows → Blast, and GW's *Penetrating Attack* also appears as an
  Acolyte Jin card.
- **Mind Spark:** GW energy denial (*Energy Burn*) → Power Drain, a strain effect.
- **Ash Walker:** *Life Siphon* → its Recover surges.

**Conventions for suggested mappings:**
- MMO energy → **strain**.
- Knockdown → **Stun**.
- Bleeding/Burning → **Bleed**.
- Poison/Disease → **Weaken**.
- "Hard mode" elite skills → **elite-variant abilities**.

### Recurring patterns worth a shared mechanic

- **Mounted units:** the Dolyak Rider, the Summit Beastmaster, and Dagnar on the Ice
  Drake. All are large figures, and the MMO gives them **Stun Immunity**. That's a
  natural shared keyword: "Mounted: large figure, cannot be Stunned."
- **Siege weapons:** Fort Ranik's trebuchets, and the ballistae at Borlis Pass and the
  Frost Gate. A reusable siege-object rule would serve three missions: an object with
  Health that fires a Blast attack each round while crewed.
- **Enchantment and hex removal:** the Sage, Howler, Chaot, Shaman and Dolyak Rider all
  do it. In IA terms, these are "discard a beneficial condition from a hostile figure"
  and "discard a harmful condition from a friendly figure". The MMO's enchantments
  mostly map onto hero class cards, so an even crueller version would be "exhaust one
  of the target's class cards". That's strong; treat it with care.
- **Interrupts:** Distracting Shot, Disrupting Chop and Power Block. A useful shared
  enemy-side mechanic, and the mesmer identity Norgu is built around.
